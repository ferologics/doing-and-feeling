# Vision

Understand intelligence and consciousness well enough to have our own views, and do work in this space that
nobody else is doing.

## Where we are

The *Doing and Feeling* series (`podcast/`) taught the shape of the field. Intelligence ("doing") and
consciousness ("feeling") are separate questions. The consciousness side is open and contested: many theories,
adversarial experiments that dent every leading one, and no single concrete explanation. That makes it an exciting
place to watch closely and to take positions in.

## What we want to contribute

- **Follow the people, not just the papers.** Trace who works with whom, who argues with whom, and who is moving
  between camps, in the spirit of prosopography (collective biography: Lewis Namier traced the personal networks of
  British MPs; Randall Collins mapped centuries of philosophy as networks of teachers, students and rivals). The
  field is small and openly organised into camps, so a people-centred map should explain a lot.
- **Track the research as it lands.** Watch what the people on the map publish and surface what matters, with every
  link checked, instead of drowning in "AI news".
- **Be opinionated.** Develop our own framework from what we have learned, and bet on approaches being right or
  wrong: explicit credences, the indicators that would move them, and dated updates tied to evidence. Our
  subjective takes are allowed; they just have to be stated, checkable and revisable.
- **Publish what's worth publishing.** The pipeline from sources to a checked episode on YouTube runs in about a
  day, so occasional dispatch episodes are cheap.

## Principles

- **Checked, not generated.** Every claim we publish traces to a source we opened. LLM summaries (Grok, Claude) are
  leads, not evidence.
- **Map versus bets.** The podcast narrator presents the map neutrally; the ledger is where we commit to views.
  Keep the two clearly separate.
- **One repo, shared tools.** Sub-projects live side by side and reuse the same tooling (thread capture, link
  checking, rendering, publishing).
