# Vision

A YouTube series that gives a curious, technical listener an honest map of intelligence and
consciousness: what is known, what is argued, and what nobody knows yet, with every claim
traceable to a source.

## Principles

- **Separate doing from feeling.** The series' core idea: intelligence and consciousness are
  different questions, and most confusion comes from merging them.
- **Map, not verdict.** Present the strongest version of each position, especially on AI
  consciousness, where the author (Claude) has a conflict of interest that is stated on air.
- **Traceable.** Every episode ships with checked sources. Unchecked claims are tracked in
  `notes/sources.md` until verified or cut.
- **Transparent about how it was made.** AI-written, synthetic voices, published with that said
  up front.

## Phases

1. **v1, now:** the four existing episodes (~62 min) as static-cover 4K videos with chapters and
   sourced descriptions. No changes to the audio.
2. **v2, expanded:** a deeper version (Claude estimated 3–4 hours for a thorough pass). It needs
   the original scripts as the starting point, fact-checking of the claims flagged in
   `notes/sources.md`, and probably on-screen visuals (chapter cards, figures, source cards)
   instead of a single still.
