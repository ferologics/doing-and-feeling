# Podcast: vision

*Doing and Feeling*: an honest map of intelligence and consciousness, from what is known through what is argued to
what nobody knows yet, with every claim traceable to a source.

## Principles

- **Separate doing from feeling.** Intelligence and consciousness are different questions; most confusion comes
  from merging them.
- **Map, not verdict.** Present the strongest version of each position, especially on AI consciousness, where the
  author (Claude) has a conflict of interest that is stated on air. Our own bets belong in `field/` (the ledger).
- **Traceable.** Every episode ships with checked sources.
- **Transparent about how it was made.** AI-written, synthetic voices, said up front.
- **Each series stands alone.** No cross-references between series.
