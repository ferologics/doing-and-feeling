How do you run experiments on something private? Episode 3 of Doing and Feeling covers what the science of consciousness can actually show: the hunt for neural correlates, blindsight and split brains, what Libet's experiments do and don't say about free will, hidden awareness in unresponsive patients, the "zap and zip" consciousness meter, and the main families of theory (global workspace, higher-order, recurrent processing, integrated information, predictive processing, attention schema, unlimited associative learning). It ends with the 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration, a preregistered head-to-head test of global workspace theory and IIT whose results challenged both.
