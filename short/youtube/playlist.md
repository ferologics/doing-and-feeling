# Playlist

## Title

```
Doing and Feeling: A Listening Guide to Intelligence and Consciousness
```

## Description

```
Four episodes, about an hour in total: what intelligence is (doing), why consciousness is a hard problem (feeling), what the science can measure, and whether an AI could feel anything. Written by Claude (Anthropic) and voiced by two synthetic voices, built from Blaise Agüera y Arcas's What Is Intelligence? and a reading-list thread on X. Published as-is; sources are in each episode's description.
```

## Order

1. What Is Intelligence? Psychometrics, Prediction & Octopuses | Doing and Feeling 1/4
2. The Hard Problem of Consciousness, and a Map of the Positions | Doing and Feeling 2/4
3. Can Science Measure Consciousness? Experiments and Theories | Doing and Feeling 3/4
4. Could an AI Be Conscious? The Arguments, and Where to Read Next | Doing and Feeling 4/4
