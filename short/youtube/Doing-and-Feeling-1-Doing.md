Episode 1 of Doing and Feeling covers the "doing" half of the mind: the three main ways intelligence gets defined (IQ and the g factor, generality and how fast you learn, prediction), Blaise Agüera y Arcas's argument that life is computation and intelligence is prediction, intelligence without brains (octopuses, Michael Levin's two-headed flatworms), how brains map space, and what we actually know about how language models work.
