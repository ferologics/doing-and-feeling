## Description

```
Could an AI feel anything? Episode 4 of Doing and Feeling lays out the arguments rather than a verdict: the indicator-properties approach (Butlin, Long et al.), Chalmers on whether a large language model could be conscious, recent work on global workspaces and introspection inside language models, Anil Seth's biological naturalism and IIT against computational functionalism, why you can't just ask the model (the gaming problem), "seemingly conscious AI", and the risks of getting it wrong in either direction. It ends with a reading path through everything in the series.

A note on the obvious conflict of interest: this episode was written by Claude, which is exactly the kind of system it discusses, and some of the research it cites comes from Anthropic, the company that makes Claude. The episode says so on air.

About this series: I asked Claude to make sense of a big X thread where people traded their best reading on consciousness, plus Blaise Agüera y Arcas's book What Is Intelligence?, and to turn it into something I could listen to. This is what it made, published as-is. Claude (Anthropic) wrote the script and made the cover art; the two voices are synthetic (Kokoro TTS). Claude ran three fact-check passes and an independent script review before rendering, but this is still an AI-made overview. Treat it as a map and check the sources below before quoting anything. When the hosts say "you" or "your thread", they mean me: it was made for one listener. Made in October 2026, so "this year" means 2026.
```
