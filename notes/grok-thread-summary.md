Here’s a compiled, categorized list of the main resources recommended in the replies to [@willdepue](https://x.com/willdepue)’s post. I prioritized linked or clearly identifiable books, papers, essays, and videos (the thread has ~110 replies, so this captures the substantive ones rather than every casual suggestion).

### Classic Philosophy & the Hard Problem
- **Thomas Nagel – “What Is It Like to Be a Bat?”** (1974 essay) — Short (~16 pages) foundational piece arguing that subjective experience (qualia) resists full physical or functional description. Widely called a must-read.
- **David Chalmers – *The Conscious Mind*** — Core statement of the hard problem, philosophical zombies, and property dualism.
- **Ludwig Wittgenstein** — Blue Book + *Philosophical Investigations* §§412–427. Analytic treatment of consciousness and private experience.
- **Douglas Hofstadter – *Gödel, Escher, Bach*** — Self-reference, strange loops, and the emergence of mind (multiple mentions).

### Neuroscience & Empirical Overviews
- **Anil Seth – *Being You: A New Science of Consciousness*** — Accessible modern account emphasizing predictive processing and the brain as a “controlled hallucination.” Frequently recommended as a strong science starting point.
- **Christof Koch – *The Feeling of Life Itself*** — Combines experimental neuroscience with Integrated Information Theory (IIT). Argues consciousness is the “feeling of being alive,” widespread in animals, but not purely computational. Called a must-read even by people who don’t fully endorse IIT.
- **Stanislas Dehaene – *Consciousness and the Brain*** — Global Neuronal Workspace Theory + key experiments on access vs. phenomenal consciousness.
- **Michael Graziano – *Rethinking Consciousness*** — Attention Schema Theory.
- **Steve Fleming – *Know Thyself*** — Metacognition and awareness of awareness.
- **Andy Clark – *The Experience Machine*** — Predictive processing framework.
- **Peter Godfrey-Smith – *Other Minds*** — Evolutionary and comparative angle (especially octopuses).

One reply suggested a “tasting menu” approach with several of the above rather than going deep on a single theory first.

### Challenges to Pure Computational Functionalism
- **Anil Seth – “Conscious artificial intelligence and biological naturalism”** (Behavioral and Brain Sciences target article, with ~50 commentaries). Argues consciousness depends on biological/metabolic organization rather than computation alone. High-profile and recently discussed in the thread.
- **Philip Goff & Sean Carroll podcast** — “Philip Goff on Consciousness Everywhere.” Formative discussion of panpsychism vs. physicalism. [Apple Podcasts link](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/philip-goff-on-consciousness-everywhere/id1406534739?i=1000455896785).
- **Julian Jaynes theory** — Bicameral mind overview: [julianjaynes.org](https://www.julianjaynes.org/about/about-jaynes-theory/overview/).
- **Bernardo Kastrup – *Why Materialism Is Baloney*** — Analytic idealism counterpoint.

### Computational / AI / Functionalist Directions
- Papers recommended by @leothecurious:
  - Active inference theory of consciousness (“A beautiful loop”)
  - “Why Is Anything Conscious?”
  - Adversarial testing of Global Neuronal Workspace vs. Integrated Information Theory
  - Hard Problem + Free Energy Principle
  - “Could a Large Language Model be Conscious?”
  - “A global workspace in language models”
- **Michael Timothy Bennett** — PhD thesis “How to Build Conscious Machines” + YouTube TLDR summary.
- **arXiv:2607.15883** — “Perceived AGI: Believability as Dimensional Completeness, Not Capability” (focuses on engineering the *perception* of mind rather than claiming actual machine consciousness).

### Talks, Videos & Channels
- **Joscha Bach** — Lex Fridman episode #101 and AGI-24 talk (computational/functionalist perspective; multiple strong recommendations).
- **Artem Kirsanov YouTube series** — Detailed, engaging videos on grid cells, place cells, brain representations, memory/engrams, artificial hippocampus, and related neuroscience (one reply listed ~8 specific videos).
- Essentia Foundation YouTube channel (non-materialist / idealism-leaning content).
- One reply pointed to a specific YouTube video as “Probably all you need” (search the thread for the exact link).

### Speculative / Alternative

- Dzogchen/Zen-oriented: *The Natural Bliss of Being* by Jackson Peterson.
