# Field: vision

A people-centred view of consciousness science and AI consciousness research: who the researchers are, how they
connect, what they publish, and what we think is right.

## Pieces, in order

1. **Map.** Every researcher who matters to the questions in *Doing and Feeling*, with their camp (global workspace,
   IIT, predictive processing, illusionism, biological naturalism, …) and their answer to the crux question of the
   series: is the right computation enough for consciousness? The edges are co-authorship (from OpenAlex), then
   argument links (BBS target articles and commentaries, adversarial collaborations), then lab affiliations. The
   point is to see the camps, the bridges between them, and who is moving.
2. **Watch.** A recurring digest of new work by the people on the map, grouped by person, each link checked.
   Following people is a better filter than following topics.
3. **Ledger.** Our credences on positions and on concrete, resolvable questions (e.g. "the next Cogitate round
   favours IIT"), with what would change our minds and dated updates tied to the evidence that caused them. Kept in
   git, so the history of our thinking is public and checkable.
4. **Scorecard.** The theory-derived indicators of consciousness (Butlin, Long et al.) assessed against frontier AI
   models, updated per model release and per interpretability paper.
5. **Dispatches.** Occasional episodes built from the watch and the ledger, through the `podcast/` pipeline.

## Principles

- Positions on the map come from the researchers' own published statements. The crux tag always carries a source; first-pass camp tags from the series stay marked unreviewed until each has one.
- The ledger is opinionated on purpose; the map is not.
- Data first from open sources (OpenAlex, arXiv, PhilPapers, journal pages); no scraping behind logins.
